What this data can't tell us

    What we'd want to collect next

    • Location / country, if the top creators cluster in one city or the US broadly, that opens in-person activation: events, meetups, studio visits, not just remote deals.
    • Follower count, to separate reach on this one trending batch from actual audience size, and catch creators whose follower base doesn't match their trending performance.
    • Account age / history, to tell an overnight spike from a creator who's been steadily building.
    • Real content category, a proper taxonomy instead of noisy hashtags, so we can match creators to specific campaign types.
